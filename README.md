# 🛒 Power BI E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales, revenue, freight costs, customer reviews, and product performance using **Microsoft Power BI**. Using the Brazilian e-commerce dataset from Olist, I developed an interactive dashboard to explore business performance, identify top-performing product categories, and gain meaningful insights from sales data.

The dashboard consists of **three interactive pages**: Sales Overview, Map Visuals, and Product Details. Each page presents important business metrics through interactive visualizations, charts, and KPI cards.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall e-commerce sales and revenue performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories based on orders and revenue.
- Track sales trends across different years and quarters.
- Analyze geographical sales distribution using map visualizations.
- Evaluate product prices, freight costs, and customer review scores.
- Build an interactive dashboard to support data-driven business decisions.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Overview
Provides a summary of overall business performance through key performance indicators (KPIs) and interactive charts.

**Key Metrics:**
- Total Orders: 98.7K
- Total Sales: $13.6M
- Total Freight: $2.25M
- Total Revenue: $15.8M
- Delivery Orders: 96K
- Cancelled Orders: 461

**Visualizations:**
- Orders by Product Category
- Revenue by Product Category
- Total Sales by Year and Quarter
- KPI cards for overall business performance

### 2. Geographical Sales Analysis
Uses map visualization to explore the geographical distribution of seller activity and sales across different locations.

**Key Features:**
- Geographic distribution of sellers.
- Location-based analysis using map visuals.
- Identification of geographical patterns in seller activity.

### 3. Product Details
Provides a detailed analysis of product categories, pricing, freight, and customer satisfaction.

**Key Metrics:**
- Total Products: 33K
- Average Review Score: 4.1
- Average Product Price: $120.7
- Average Freight Cost: $20.0

**Visualizations:**
- Top 10 Products by Revenue
- Average Review Score by Product Category
- Price and Freight Scatter Plot
- Category-wise Orders, Sales, Revenue, and Freight Table
